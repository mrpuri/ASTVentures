Questions

Frequently asked

Do we need existing computer labs to start?

No. We assess what you have and specify the smallest viable setup that will actually support the curriculum. Many schools start with a single room and expand once teachers are confident.

Who teaches the classes — your staff or ours?

Both models exist. For schools we usually train your own teachers and certify them, with our support in the classroom during the first term. For college and university programs, an AST trainer leads the course while your faculty are trained alongside — as we do on the blockchain course at Atria University.

What is Intellistudio, and who is it for?

Our AI platform for manufacturers. It connects to the systems you already use and automates the repetitive work across production, sales, procurement, finance and billing. It works alongside your existing software rather than replacing it. See how it works →

Can you help us open a centre in Punjab?

Yes — that is what our GCC practice does, usually on a build–operate–transfer basis, or as a smaller pilot pod first. How a Punjab GCC works →

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need — an education program, an Intellistudio deployment or a capability centre — and on what already exists. Tell us where you are and we will send a written proposal with the full breakdown.

How does the NASSCOM certification work?

NASSCOM issues certificates in select areas under a prior agreement. Which of your programs qualify is confirmed in writing before enrolment opens, so nobody is promised a credential that has not been signed off.
