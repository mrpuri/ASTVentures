The gap

NEP opened the door. Most colleges have nobody to teach it.

Multidisciplinary and skill-based credits are permitted — but AI and blockchain need people who work with these tools, and those people are not usually on a college payroll. That is the part we supply.

Mapped to your credits

Courses structured to fit your existing credit and semester framework under NEP 2020 — as a skill enhancement course, an elective or a minor track.

Taught by practitioners

The trainer in the room works with the technology. Students get current tooling and real workflows, not slides written three syllabi ago.

Your faculty trained too

Nominated faculty sit through delivery and get a train-the-trainer track, so by the second or third cohort the course can run in-house.

Getting started

From first call to first class

1

Academic fit

We look at your programs, the credit slots available under NEP and which cohort makes sense to start with. Usually one course, one semester.

2

Course design

You get the syllabus, session plan, lab requirements, assessment scheme and the trainer's profile in writing before anything is approved.

3

Delivery

Our trainer runs the course on your campus to your calendar, with your nominated faculty in the room throughout.

4

Handover or extend

Review the cohort's outcomes, then either certify your faculty to run it in-house or extend to the next course in the track.

Next step

Talk to us about a course

Tell us your programs and the semester you are planning for, and we will come back with a syllabus, a session plan and what it costs.

Start with one course and one cohort. It is far easier to prove the model on a single semester than to negotiate a whole track on a slide.
