The thesis

The proof exists. The infrastructure doesn't.

Three models have already been proven at scale, in three different countries. None of them has been built for tier-2 and tier-3 India, in its own languages. That is the gap AST Ventures exists to close.

State infrastructure

China made AI education state infrastructure — mandatory K–12 AI curriculum and a national network of designated AI base schools.

The AI-native school

The United States proved the model — campuses where AI tutors compress academics into hours and free the rest of the day for building real things.

Centres that scale

China's Squirrel AI proved that physical AI-learning centres scale as a network — thousands of them, in cities most tech companies never visit.

India has now issued its own mandate. AI compulsory in CBSE schools from 2026–27. 50,000 new tinkering labs funded. Roughly ten million teachers to be trained. The opportunity is identical — and almost nobody is building the delivery infrastructure. We are. Starting in Jalandhar.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do we need existing computer labs to start?

No. We assess what you have and specify the smallest viable setup that will actually support the curriculum. Many schools start with a single room and expand once teachers are confident.

Who teaches the classes — your staff or ours?

Both models exist. For schools we usually train your own teachers and certify them, with our support in the classroom during the first term. For college and university programs, an AST trainer leads the course while your faculty are trained alongside — as we do on the blockchain course at Atria University.

Is this only for Punjab?

Punjab is where we build first and prove the model, starting from Jalandhar. We already deliver well beyond it — a blockchain course under NEP at Atria University in Bengaluru, and sessions at NIFT Panchkula, GNA University and LPU.

What does it cost?

Commercials depend on the model, the size of the cohort and what infrastructure already exists. Tell us what you have and we will send a written proposal with the full breakdown.

How does the NASSCOM certification work?

NASSCOM issues certificates in select areas under a prior agreement. Which of your programs qualify is confirmed in writing before enrolment opens, so nobody is promised a credential that has not been signed off.

For parents

The questions parents actually ask

Will this take time away from board exams?

Young AI Innovators runs on weekends and is deliberately structured around exam calendars. We reduce load in board years rather than pretending it does not exist.

My child has never coded. Is that a problem?

No. The Explorer level assumes nothing. Students start with tools and thinking, and code arrives once they have something they want to build.

Is a six-month program really enough for a job?

It is enough to be employable if the six months are spent building rather than watching. That is why the program is full time, cohort based and portfolio-assessed. We are honest with applicants about effort — this is not a part-time certificate.

What are the fees?

Fees are shared on enquiry and instalment options are available. Send us an enquiry and we will send the full fee structure with no obligation.

Do you teach in Punjabi?

Yes. Instruction and support are available in English, Hindi and Punjabi. Technical terms stay in English because that is the language of the tools and the interviews.

What we believe

Four convictions the company is built on

Talent is evenly distributed. Infrastructure is not.

Punjab has never lacked capable people. It has lacked labs that work, teachers trained on current tools, and a visible path from a classroom here to a career anywhere.

Delivery beats content.

There is no shortage of AI courses on the internet. There is a severe shortage of rooms where a trained teacher runs a good class on Tuesday morning. We build the rooms.

Language is not a detail.

Instruction in a language that is not yours is a tax on learning. We teach in English, Hindi and Punjabi, and keep the technical vocabulary in English because that is what the tools and interviews use.

Compliance is a floor, not a goal.

The mandate can be met on paper very cheaply. We are not interested in that. The measure is whether students leave with work they built and can show.
