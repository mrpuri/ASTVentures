Where the hours go

Most of a factory's office work is moving information around.

The machines are often well run. The paperwork around them usually is not — and it is where time and cash quietly leak out.

Typing the same thing twice

An order arrives on WhatsApp, goes into a spreadsheet, then into accounts, then into a dispatch register. Every hop is a person and a chance for a mistake.

Chasing, not deciding

Managers spend their day asking for status — what's pending, what's dispatched, who hasn't paid — because the answer lives in five places.

Billing that runs late

Invoices and running account bills wait on measurements, approvals and paperwork. Every day a bill waits is a day your cash sits with the customer.

How it works

Connect. Understand. Automate.

1

Connect

Intellistudio links to the systems you already run — accounting, ERP, spreadsheets, email and messaging. Nothing is ripped out; nobody learns new software to start.

2

3

Automate

AI agents take over the repetitive work: compiling reports, drafting documents, matching records, following up. People approve; they stop retyping.

Questions

What manufacturers ask first

Do we have to replace our accounting software or ERP?

No. Intellistudio works alongside what you already use and connects to it. Your accounts team keeps working in the tools they know; the retyping between them is what goes away.

Do our people need to be technical?

No. Staff ask questions and approve work in plain language — English, Hindi or Punjabi. We train every user as part of the rollout.

What size of manufacturer is this for?

It is built for small and mid-sized manufacturers — the plants where a handful of office staff hold the whole business together and every hour they get back matters.

Where does our data live, and who can see it?

Deployment, access by role and data handling are agreed with you in writing before anything is connected. Your data is used to run your company brain, for your business.

How quickly do we see a result?

We start with a single workflow precisely so the result is visible early and measurable against how you work today. The discovery session sets the timeline and the yardstick before anything goes live.
