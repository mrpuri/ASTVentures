Fit

Who this works for

This is an operating business, not a passive investment. The partners who do well are the ones present in the centre.

  • Education entrepreneurs already running coaching, tuition or skilling centres
  • School or college groups wanting a commercial AI arm alongside academics
  • Operators with local credibility in a tier-2 or tier-3 city
  • Returning professionals who want to build something at home rather than elsewhere
We do not award a licence to every applicant. Territory, operating capability and commitment to teaching quality all matter more to us than speed.
