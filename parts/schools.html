Curriculum

What students actually do

Every stage ends with something built. Not a chapter finished — a thing that works, that the student can show a parent.

  • Grades 6–8: computational thinking, data literacy, first AI tools, block-based building
  • Grades 9–10: Python foundations, data handling, applied machine-learning projects, AI ethics
  • Grades 11–12: model building, generative AI and prompting, deployment, capstone project
  • Across all stages: tinkering-lab activities, competitions and an annual school AI showcase
